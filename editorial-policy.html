Editorial policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Sources

Rules, figures and dates come from primary sources: canada.ca, provincial government sites, the Canada Gazette and official data files. Where we rely on reporting for context, we confirm the fact against the primary source before publishing. Each guide links to the official page for its topic.

Dates

Every guide carries a review date. Figures that change, such as cut-off scores, fees and targets, are shown with the date they took effect or were measured. Draw data is refreshed automatically from IRCC's public rounds file and shows its refresh date.

Tools

Calculators implement the published rules as written. When a rule changes we update the tool and note the change on the page. Tools state plainly that results are estimates.

What we will not publish

  • Predictions presented as fact, including forecast cut-off scores.
  • Guarantees of immigration outcomes.
  • Advice on concealing or misstating information in an application.
  • Unlabelled paid content.

Independence

Advertisers, sponsors and referral partners have no say over guides, data or tool logic. Sponsored content is labelled. Referral partners are licensed professionals whom we verify against their regulator's public register.

Use of automation

Data collection is automated. Drafting and review may be assisted by software, including AI tools; a person is responsible for checking every published fact against its source.

Corrections

If you find an error, tell us through the contact form and choose Correction. We fix confirmed errors promptly and update the review date. Significant corrections are noted on the page.
