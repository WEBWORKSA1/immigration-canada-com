What people ask before sending

Does this cost anything?

No. The assessment is free. If you later choose to hire a consultant or lawyer, they set their own fees and you deal with them directly.

Who sees my information?

The site team. We share it with a licensed professional only if you tick the optional box. We never sell contact lists. Details are in the privacy policy.

Can you guarantee a visa or a job?

No, and nobody honestly can. Decisions are made only by IRCC or a province. Be wary of anyone who promises approval.

Can I apply without help?

Yes. Every application can be made directly through canada.ca. Our guides and tools are built for people doing exactly that.
