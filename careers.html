Work with us

We are building the most useful independent resource on Canadian immigration. We work with people on a freelance, per-project basis, from anywhere.

Freelance

Writers with lived experience

You went through Express Entry, a provincial program, a study permit or sponsorship and can explain it precisely, with sources.

Professional

Licensed reviewers and partners

RCICs and Canadian immigration lawyers in good standing who review guides or receive assessment referrals in their practice area.

Creator

Video creators

Short, accurate explainers and first-person settlement videos for our library and channels.

Technical

Developers and data people

Provincial points calculators, occupation lookups and data pipelines from official sources.

Language

Translators

French first, then the languages our readers use most.

Growth

Community and outreach

People who run newcomer groups, forums or newsletters and want to collaborate.

How we work

  • Accuracy before speed. Every factual claim needs an official source.
  • No guarantees, no fear-based selling, no advice we are not licensed to give.
  • Paid per piece or per project, agreed in writing before work starts.
  • Licensed partners are verified against their regulator's public register before any referral.
