How to read Express Entry rounds

IRCC holds three kinds of rounds. Program-specific rounds invite candidates eligible for one program, such as the Canadian Experience Class or the Provincial Nominee Program. Category-based rounds invite candidates with a particular attribute, such as French-language ability or experience in healthcare, trades or transport. General rounds invite the top-ranked candidates across all programs; the last one was held in April 2024.

Cut-offs are only comparable within a round type. A Provincial Nominee Program cut-off in the 700s includes the 600 points every nominee receives, so the underlying score is in the 100s to 200s. French-language rounds have had the lowest regular cut-offs in 2026, between 382 and 435, because IRCC is working toward a target share of French-speaking admissions outside Quebec (9.5% for 2027, rising to 12% in 2029).

When several candidates sit exactly on the cut-off, IRCC applies a tie-breaking rule based on the date and time each profile was submitted, so entering the pool earlier can matter.

After the November 2026 rule change

On 3 November 2026 many candidates with Canadian experience or job offers in high-wage occupations gained points. Because the whole pool was rescored at once, the first cut-offs after the pause are not a like-for-like continuation of the earlier trend. Treat pre-November cut-offs as context, not a forecast.
