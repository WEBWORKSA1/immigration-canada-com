Pathway Finder

Eleven questions. Ranked results with the reason each program fits, based on published eligibility rules.

How this works

The finder compares your answers with the headline requirements of each program and ranks the matches. It does not check every condition, such as admissibility, proof of funds or occupation lists.

For a points estimate, use the CRS calculator.

Nothing you enter here is stored or sent anywhere. It runs in your browser.
