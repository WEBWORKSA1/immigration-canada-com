CLB Language Converter

Canadian immigration programs set language requirements in Canadian Language Benchmarks. Enter your test scores to see the CLB level for each skill.

Results must be less than two years old when you apply. For French, IRCC accepts TEF Canada and TCF Canada; see the official equivalency charts for NCLC levels.

Minimum score for each CLB level

Your level is set separately for each skill. Programs and CRS bonuses look at your lowest skill, so one weak band can cost many points.

Which CLB level do you need?

  • Federal Skilled Worker: CLB 7 in all four skills.
  • Canadian Experience Class: CLB 7 for TEER 0 or 1 occupations, CLB 5 for TEER 2 or 3.
  • Federal Skilled Trades: CLB 5 for speaking and listening, CLB 4 for reading and writing.
  • Maximum CRS language points: CLB 10 in each skill. CLB 9 in all four unlocks the highest skill-transferability bonuses.
  • Citizenship: CLB 4 in speaking and listening for applicants aged 18 to 54.
