Contests and prizes

We run community contests that reward useful, honest accounts of moving to Canada, with prizes aimed at the costs applicants actually face.

Status: no contest is open for entries right now. Leave your email below and we will tell you when the next one opens. We never announce winners or prizes anywhere except this page and by direct email to entrants.

Contest formats

Writing

Newcomer Story Awards

Tell the story of one decision in your immigration journey and what you would do differently. Judged on usefulness to the next applicant, accuracy and clarity. Selected entries are published with the author's permission.

Skill

Know-the-Rules Challenge

A timed quiz on current program rules, built from the guides on this site. Top scorers are entered into the prize round.

Video

First 90 Days on Camera

A short video with one practical tip for a new arrival. Open to creators of any size.

Prizes

Prizes are designed to offset real costs, for example a language-test fee, an educational credential assessment, or settlement essentials. The prize, its value, the number of winners and the closing date are published here when each contest opens. Prizes are funded by reader contributions and labelled sponsors.

Standard rules

  • No purchase necessary. Entry is free, and contributing or donating does not improve your chances.
  • Eligibility. Open to adults who have reached the age of majority where they live, except where prohibited by law. Each contest states any country or province restrictions.
  • Selection. Skill contests are judged against published criteria. Where a prize is awarded by random draw to a Canadian resident, the selected entrant must correctly answer a skill-testing question.
  • No immigration benefit. Winning has no effect on any immigration application, and we cannot influence one.
  • Privacy. Entry details are used only to run the contest, under our privacy policy.
  • Official rules. Each contest has its own full rules page, which prevails over this summary.
