Common questions

Is this a Government of Canada website?

No. immigration-canada.com is a privately owned, independent information site. The only official source for applications, forms and fees is canada.ca, run by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). We link to it throughout.

What changed in Express Entry on 3 November 2026?

IRCC added a Labour Market Integration factor to the Comprehensive Ranking System. Canadian work experience keeps its existing points (up to 80), and experience in occupations on the high-wage list earns up to 70 more. Alternatively, a qualifying high-wage job offer earns 50, 70 or 100 points. You get whichever option scores higher, and a job offer no longer needs an LMIA to count.

Why are there no new rounds right now?

IRCC said it would pause invitation rounds while it recalculates the score of every profile in the pool, a process it estimated at up to two weeks from 3 November 2026. Our draw tracker updates itself when rounds resume.

Do I need to pay someone to apply?

No. You can apply on your own through canada.ca. If you do pay for advice or representation, the person must be a lawyer, a Quebec notary, or a consultant licensed by the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants. See how to check.
