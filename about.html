About this site

immigration-canada.com is an independent, privately owned website that helps people understand their options for moving to Canada and measure where they stand.

What we do

  • Build free tools. A CRS calculator kept current with rule changes, a draw tracker that refreshes from IRCC data, a pathway finder, a language converter and a cost calculator.
  • Write plain-language guides to the main programs, each with a review date and a link to the official source.
  • Track the changes that matter and summarise them briefly.
  • Offer a free assessment and, with consent, introduce readers to licensed professionals.

What we do not do

  • We are not the Government of Canada, IRCC or any provincial authority, and we are not affiliated with or endorsed by them.
  • We are not a law firm or an immigration consultancy. We do not give legal advice, prepare applications or represent anyone.
  • We do not sell visas, job offers or guaranteed outcomes. Nobody legitimately can.
  • We do not charge readers to use the tools or guides.

How the site is funded

The site is supported by advertising (Google AdSense), sponsorships, referral arrangements with licensed professionals, and voluntary contributions from readers. Advertisers and partners do not review or influence our guides, calculators or data. Sponsored material is labelled. See the editorial policy.

How we keep information accurate

Figures such as cut-off scores, fees and targets come from official publications and are dated on the page. Draw data refreshes automatically. Rules change often; if a page and canada.ca disagree, canada.ca is right, and we would like to hear about it through the contact form.
