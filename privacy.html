Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information immigration-canada.com (the site, we) collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

  • Free assessment form: your goal, preferred province, timeline, age, education, language test status, work experience, occupation, optional CRS score, name, email, optional phone number, country of residence and any message.
  • Contact, newsletter, contest, contribution and work-with-us forms: the details you enter, such as name, email and message.

We use this information to reply to you, provide the assessment you asked for, send emails you subscribed to, run contests you entered and manage contributions. We share assessment details with a licensed immigration consultant or lawyer only if you tick the optional consent box. We do not sell personal information.

Please do not send passport numbers, identity documents, health information or details of an existing application through our forms. We do not need them.

Information the tools use

The CRS calculator, pathway finder, language converter and cost calculator run entirely in your browser. What you enter is not sent to us. The site stores a few small items in your browser's local storage: your last CRS score (to pre-fill other tools), your cookie-notice choice, and cached draw data. You can clear these in your browser settings.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info or youradchoices.ca.
  • Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent to advertising cookies through a Google-certified consent message where required.

Service providers

  • Hosting: GitHub Pages, which logs IP addresses for security.
  • Forms: submissions are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit, a form-to-email service.
  • Video: YouTube embeds use the privacy-enhanced domain and set cookies only when you play a video.
  • Fonts: Google Fonts.
  • Advertising: Google AdSense.

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States.

Retention

We keep form submissions for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a record of consent, normally no longer than 24 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Canada's PIPEDA, Quebec's Law 25, the GDPR and US state privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to unsubscribe from emails at any time. Use the contact form and choose Privacy request. We will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local privacy regulator.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.
