The Labour Market Integration factor

This factor replaced the stand-alone Canadian work experience row. It has two options, and IRCC applies whichever gives you more points:

  • Canadian work experience. The familiar length-of-experience points (40 for one year up to 80 for five years, without a spouse) plus a bonus for at least 12 months in listed high-wage occupations: 20 points where the occupation's median wage is at least 1.3 times the average, 40 points at 1.5 times, 70 points at 2 times.
  • Qualifying job offer. 50, 70 or 100 points for a valid offer in a high-wage occupation, by the same wage tiers. An LMIA is no longer required, and an offer outside the high-wage list earns no CRS points, although it can still help with program eligibility.

Skill-transferability rules did not change. Twelve months means 1,560 hours, which can be part-time or split across jobs.

What moves a score most

  1. Provincial nomination: 600 points.
  2. Language: the step from CLB 8 to CLB 9 in all four abilities is worth up to 32 core points and can double your skill-transferability points.
  3. French: NCLC 7 adds 25 or 50 points and opens French-language rounds.
  4. High-wage Canadian experience or job offer: up to 70 or 100 points since November 2026.
  5. A second credential or Canadian study: 15 to 30 additional points.
